# NERve 5-Minute Judge Demo

## 1. Explain the operational hierarchy

Open three tabs or devices using the State Control, District Operations and Field Officer URLs. Explain that each government level receives only relevant information, while P1 alerts travel across all affected levels.

## 2. Explain village priority

Open **Priority Villages** and expand the first village.

Say: “NERve does not rank a village using population alone. It balances medical urgency, isolation time, food and water remaining, hazard exposure, population and alternate access. Every point is visible and the final decision remains with an authorised official.”

## 3. Run the animated mission

Open **Relief Planner** and press **Start Judge Demo Mission**.

Show the warehouse, truck leg, river transfer hub, boat leg, landing point, last-mile 4×4 and destination. Click markers to show vehicle IDs and departure/arrival times. Point out that demo coordinates and timings are clearly labelled simulated.

## 4. Demonstrate inventory control

Press **Approve & reserve stock**, then open **Warehouses** to show physical, reserved and available quantities. Return to the plan and press **Dispatch & deduct stock**. Open Warehouses again and show the transaction ledger.

Say: “Drafts do not alter inventory. Approval reserves stock, dispatch deducts it, and cancellation releases a reservation. This prevents double allocation.”

## 5. Demonstrate disruption replanning

Open **Simulate & Replan**, choose ROAD and a 45-minute reporting delay, then run the scenario. Show the grey original route, blocked marker, green revised route and ETA difference. Press **Approve revised plan & broadcast**.

## 6. Demonstrate the historical model

Use Guwahati to Jorhat with a departure time and press **Predict route hazard**.

Say: “The baseline uses 93,504 historical NASA POWER feature rows. Because verified road-closure labels are unavailable, we honestly call the output a weather-hazard score—not an incident probability.”

## One-line closing

“NERve helps authorised government teams prioritise disconnected communities, reserve scarce supplies, choose multimodal transport, continue working through weak networks and safely replan when verified conditions change.”
